## 1. Executive Summary
The platform occupies the white space between **social activity loggers (Strava)**, **complex enterprise coaching platforms (TrainingPeaks)**, and **device-locked telemetry portals (Garmin Connect)**.

## 2. Competitive Landscape Comparison

| Dimension | Strava | TrainingPeaks | Garmin Connect | This Platform |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Focus** | Social feed, clubs, segment competition. | Elite coaching, complex TSS/ATL/CTL charts. | Device synchronization, hardware health. | **Personal training prescription, planned vs actual analysis.** |
| **Planning UX** | Rudimentary / non-existent on free tier. | Cluttered, 2000s desktop interface, steep learning curve. | Rigid, tied exclusively to Garmin workouts. | **Intuitive drag-and-drop, weekly focused calendar.** |
| **Comparative Engine** | None. Treats every activity as standalone. | Available, but buried in complex modal menus. | None. | **Hero feature: instant side-by-side delta visualization.** |
| **Visual Design** | Orange social feed. | Grey/blue spreadsheet aesthetic. | Dark, dense, but heavily device-centric. | **Athletic Obsidian, high density, editorial typography.** |
| **Intelligence** | Generic AI summaries ("Great effort!"). | None (requires human coach). | Rule-based Daily Suggested Workouts. | **Grounded statistical AI Coach with explicit evidence citations.** |

## 3. Unique Selling Propositions (USPs)
1. **Zero Social Friction:** No vanity metrics, follower counts, or kudos farming. Designed for pure personal athletic discipline.
2. **Transparent Science:** No black-box recovery scores; insights are tied directly to documented sports science metrics (10% rule, ACWR, pace efficiency).
3. **Strava-Native Ecosystem:** Leverages existing athlete devices (Garmin, Apple Watch, Wahoo, Coros) via Strava without forcing hardware switching.

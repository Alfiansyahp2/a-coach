## 1. Architecture Decision Records (ADR) Process
Any substantial change to data storage, third-party integrations, security protocols, or tech stack requires:
1. Creating a new ADR in `docs/03-architecture/ARCHITECTURE_DECISIONS/ADR-XXX-<TITLE>.md`.
2. Documenting Context, Options Considered, Decision, and Consequences.
3. Review and sign-off by Principal Architect.

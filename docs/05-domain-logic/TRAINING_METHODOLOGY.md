## 1. Physiological Foundations
The platform adheres to established exercise physiology principles (Daniels' VDOT, Seiler's Polarized Training, and Gabbett's Workload Paradigm):
* **The 80/20 Polarized Rule:** Approximately 80% of total weekly training volume should be accumulated at low intensity (Zone 2 Aerobic Base), with ~20% reserved for high-intensity threshold and interval stress.
* **Progressive Overload:** Musculoskeletal and cardiovascular systems adapt to gradual increases in training load. Increasing volume by $>10-15\%$ week-over-week substantially elevates risk of soft tissue injury (patellofemoral pain, Achilles tendinopathy, tibial stress fractures).
* **Supercompensation:** Training stimulus depletes glycogen and causes micro-tears; physiological adaptation occurs during subsequent rest periods. Without adequate recovery, overreaching transitions into chronic overtraining.

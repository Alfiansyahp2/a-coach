## 1. Historical Analytics Horizons
The system computes rolling aggregate trends across standard timeframes:
* **7 Days:** Microcycle focus (immediate fatigue vs freshness).
* **30 Days:** Mesocycle focus (monthly volume accumulation and adaptation).
* **90 Days:** Macrocycle base building (quarterly endurance progression).
* **1 Year:** Annual periodization (year-over-year volume and PR history).

## 2. Pace Adaptation Trendline
Evaluates aerobic efficiency by measuring moving average pace across all Zone 2 runs over time. A downward sloping curve indicates cardiac stroke volume adaptation (faster pace at identical aerobic effort).

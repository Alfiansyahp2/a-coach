## 1. Supported Workout Categories

| Category | Primary Energy System | Target Intensity Zone | Standard Prescribed Duration | Key Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Easy Run** | Aerobic Lipolysis | Zone 2 (RPE 3-4/10) | 30 - 60 mins | Mitochondrial development, capillary bed growth. |
| **Long Run** | Aerobic Base / Glycogen | Zone 2 (RPE 4-5/10) | 75 - 150 mins | Glycogen conservation, mental stamina, base endurance. |
| **Tempo Run** | Lactate Threshold | Zone 4 (RPE 7-8/10) | 40 - 70 mins | Raises blood lactate clearance velocity. |
| **Interval Training** | VO2 Max / Anaerobic | Zone 5 (RPE 9/10) | 30 - 50 mins | Cardiac stroke volume, neuromuscular speed. |
| **Recovery Run** | Active Recovery | Zone 1 (RPE 2-3/10) | 20 - 40 mins | Blood flow to aid muscular tissue repair. |
| **Fartlek** | Mixed Aerobic/Anaerobic | Zone 2 to 5 | 30 - 60 mins | Speed play, terrain adaptation. |
| **Cycling** | Low-Impact Aerobic | Zone 1 to 4 | 45 - 180 mins | Cardiovascular volume without orthopedic pounding. |
| **Swimming** | Total-Body Aerobic | Zone 2 to 4 | 30 - 75 mins | Upper body endurance and respiratory conditioning. |
| **Strength Training** | Neuromuscular / Hypertrophy | N/A | 30 - 60 mins | Injury resilience, tendon stiffness, core stability. |
| **Cross Training** | Mixed Low-Impact | Zone 2 to 3 | 30 - 60 mins | Active recovery and joint de-loading. |
| **Rest Day** | Cellular Repair | Complete Rest | 0 mins | Glycogen resynthesis, muscle fiber repair. |

## 1. Overview of the Design System

The **Endurance Performance Lab Design System** is an unapologetically rigorous, high-craft design language created for serious endurance athletes (runners and cyclists). It combines:
1. **Avionics Telemetry HUD**: High-density dashboards, monospace tabular numbers, and sharp 1px borders in Graphite Obsidian (Dark) and Chalk Lab (Light).
2. **Tactile Athletic Field Journal**: An authentic open-book physical metaphor (`AddActivityModal.tsx`) featuring real ruled/grid paper textures, a central crease, a crimson ribbon bookmark, and multi-step post-session check-in.
3. **Clinical Gatekeeper & Joint Safety**: Deterministic sports science integrating Borg CR10 perceived exertion, 3-point joint pain tracking, biological circuit breakers (effusion, laxity), and acute injury screening.
4. **Anti-AI-Slop Engineering**: Strict Clean Architecture limits (`< 300 lines` per file), zero purple/indigo SaaS gradient slop, zero proportional font jitter, and zero hallucinated diagnostics.

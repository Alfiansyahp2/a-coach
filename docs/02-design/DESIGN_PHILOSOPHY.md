## 1. Product Personality: The Quiet Craftsman Coach
The platform is not a cheerleader, a gamified cartoon, or a social feed. It embodies the quiet discipline of a world-class endurance coach holding a digital stopwatch:
* **Stoic & Direct:** Delivers honest, unflinching data. If you ran 30 seconds slower than prescribed, it displays $+30	ext{s /km}$ without judgment or fake praise.
* **Technically Precise:** Respects the athlete's intelligence. Uses exact seconds, meters, and physiological zone terminology.
* **Utilitarian Elegance:** Every pixel serves legibility. Form follows telemetry.

## 2. Emotional Tone
| Situation | Tone / UX Response |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Workout Matched Successfully** | Clean green status indicator, exact variance breakdown, quiet validation. |
| **Workout Missed or Terminated Early** | Neutral factual status (`MISSED` or `82% completed`). No shaming, no fake empathy. |
| **Volume Spike Detected** | Clear, clinical warning with supporting data (acute vs chronic mileage). |
| **Strava Disconnected / Rate Limited** | Technical explanation with clear action path and reset timestamp. |

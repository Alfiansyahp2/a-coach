## 1. Evaluated Visual Directions

### Direction 1: Obsidian Telemetry (SELECTED)
* **Character:** Pitch-black background, ultra-crisp hairline dividers, high-contrast volt and cyan accents, tabular numbers, zero rounded bubble clutter.
* **Inspiration:** Garmin Marq telemetry, WHOOP 4.0 data stream, aerospace avionics.
* **Pros:** Unmatched contrast, extremely legible in dark environments, feels professional and expensive.
* **Risks:** Requires disciplined typography and spacing to prevent looking overly harsh.

### Direction 2: Clean Editorial Slate (PROPOSED EXPLORATION)
* **Character:** Off-black charcoal background (`#18191E`), muted cream text, terracotta and sage accents, serif display headings.
* **Inspiration:** Tracksmith, Ciele Athletics, printed running journals.
* **Pros:** Highly sophisticated, warm lifestyle appeal.
* **Why Rejected for Core:** Lacks the high-contrast technical precision required for rapid glance-and-go workout comparisons.

---

## 2. Formal Selection Rationale
**Direction 1 (Obsidian Telemetry)** is selected as the authoritative visual system because it directly supports high-density analytical dashboards, renders charts with maximum contrast, and establishes a commanding athletic identity.

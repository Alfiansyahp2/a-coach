## Persona 1: Rian — The Sub-3:15 Marathoner
* **Age:** 32 | **Occupation:** Software Engineer | **Location:** Urban
* **Athletic Profile:** Runs 5-6 times per week (60-80 km/week). Uses Garmin Forerunner + HRM chest strap.
* **Goals:** Break 3 hours 15 minutes in his autumn marathon; avoid recurring Achilles tendonitis.
* **Frustrations:** Strava tells him his run was "hard", but doesn't tell him if he ran his Tuesday 10K Tempo at the prescribed 4:50 min/km or accidentally went too fast (4:35 min/km), burning matches needed for Sunday's Long Run.
* **Key Feature Needs:** Granular target pace vs actual pace comparison, 10% volume spike alerts, and ACWR monitoring.

## Persona 2: Maya — The 70.3 Triathlete
* **Age:** 28 | **Occupation:** Financial Analyst | **Location:** Suburban
* **Athletic Profile:** Trains across Swim, Bike, Run 6 days per week. Uses Wahoo bike computer and Garmin watch.
* **Goals:** Complete her first Ironman 70.3 without blowing up on the run leg.
* **Frustrations:** Disjointed tracking; Garmin Connect is heavily siloed; Strava mixes social walks with structured interval bike workouts.
* **Key Feature Needs:** Multi-sport calendar view, sport-specific volume breakdowns, and clear distinction between aerobic recovery and high-intensity threshold days.

## Persona 3: Dimas — The Habitual 5K/10K Runner
* **Age:** 24 | **Occupation:** Graphic Designer | **Location:** Urban
* **Athletic Profile:** Runs 3-4 times per week (15-25 km/week). Uses Apple Watch.
* **Goals:** Build consistent cardiovascular fitness, run 100 km in a month, avoid burnout.
* **Frustrations:** Lack of guidance on when to rest; feels guilty taking rest days; spreadsheet training plans feel tedious.
* **Key Feature Needs:** Simple calendar creation, rest day scheduling, monthly mileage progress bars, and personal record recognition.
